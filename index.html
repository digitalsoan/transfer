Privacy Policy • DPDP Act 2023

1. Direct P2P Architecture (Zero File Retention)

LocalShare operates strictly via peer-to-peer WebRTC connections. Files, media, and documents transmitted through this service travel encrypted directly between your devices or through temporary relay candidates. At no point are your files stored, logged, or retained on Digital Soan servers.

2. Compliance with Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023)

In accordance with India's DPDP Act 2023, Digital Soan acts as a Data Fiduciary adhering to lawful processing, purpose limitation, and data minimization. We only store temporary pairing PINs in memory and your local theme/cookie preferences in your browser's LocalStorage.
